## Preprint and reproducibility package

This release publishes the non-peer-reviewed preprint *Degree-Preserving Structural Perturbations and CDCL Behavior in Tseitin Formulas* and its frozen reproducibility package.

The experiments use degree-preserving double-edge perturbations of a 4-regular ring and distinguish a discovery campaign, an independently seeded confirmation, a fixed-graph presentation pilot, a prespecified repeated-presentation robustness campaign, and explicitly labelled exploratory sensitivities. The independent campaign replicated the prespecified structural associations. In the robustness sample, 24 of 60 graphs had both resolved and censored outcomes across five randomized presentations.

The adjusted global-efficiency association retained the expected negative direction, but its additional held-out predictive gain was inconclusive. Under the frozen decision rule, **robustness was not established**; this is not evidence that the association is absent.

The attached reproducibility archive contains preserved inputs, outcomes, protocols, manifests, analysis scripts, dependencies, and verification records. Its analysis-only commands do not invoke Kissat. The archive was verified against its SHA-256 sidecar, 14 numerical output snapshots reproduced byte-for-byte, and the five regenerated paper figures matched the archived renders.

The scope is limited to the studied graph size, encoding, solver, presentation distribution, and 60-second budget. The work does not establish causality, minimum proof size, exact-treewidth independence, or generalization to other sizes or solvers.
